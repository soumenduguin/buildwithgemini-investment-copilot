# Risk Profiles in Investment Copilot Simulation

> **DISCLAIMER:** *Synthetic Educational Investment Simulation Knowledge ONLY. This document defines internal rules for a fictitious educational simulation and does NOT constitute real-world financial advice or investment recommendations.*

## Overview of Simulated Risk Profiles

In the Investment Copilot simulation, an investor's risk profile determines the baseline illustrative asset allocation across five synthetic asset classes: Equity, Debt, Gold, International Equity, and Cash/Liquid.

The simulation categorizes risk tolerance into three distinct tiers:
1. Conservative
2. Moderate
3. Aggressive

---

## Conservative Risk Profile

### Simulated Risk Characteristics
- **Loss Aversion:** High tolerance for capital preservation, low tolerance for short-term market volatility.
- **Investment Horizon Orientation:** Typically paired with shorter time horizons or high capital safety preferences.
- **Growth/Defensive Balance:** 25% Growth assets / 75% Defensive & Liquid assets.

### Simulator Treatment & Baseline Allocation
When an investor profile is configured as **Conservative**, the simulator applies the following baseline illustrative allocation:
- **Debt (Fixed Income):** 55.0%
- **Equity (Domestic Equity Index):** 20.0%
- **Gold (Commodity Reserve):** 10.0%
- **Cash / Liquid:** 10.0%
- **International Equity:** 5.0%
- **Total Baseline Allocation:** 100.0%
