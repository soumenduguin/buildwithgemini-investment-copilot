## Overview of Simulated Asset Classes

The Investment Copilot portfolio simulator models illustrative allocations across **five broad asset classes**. Each asset class serves a specific structural role in balancing potential returns against hypothetical market risks.

---

## 1. Equity (Domestic Broad & Large-Cap Equity)

### Purpose within Simulation
Acts as the primary driver of long-term capital growth in the illustrative portfolio.

### Simulated Characteristics
- **Expected Volatility:** Moderate-to-High (simulated annual volatility ~14.0% – 24.8%).
- **Historical 1-Year Simulated Return:** High potential upside (simulated +11.5% to +18.2%).
- **Typical Role:** Core growth driver for Moderate (45%) and Aggressive (60%) profiles.

## 2. Debt (Fixed Income & Bonds)

### Purpose within Simulation
Provides capital stability, income generation, and downside protection during equity market stress.

### Simulated Characteristics
- **Expected Volatility:** Low (simulated annual volatility ~4.2% – 5.8%).
- **Historical 1-Year Simulated Return:** Stable conservative yield (simulated +6.1% to +7.2%).
- **Typical Role:** Core defensive foundation for Conservative (55%) and Moderate (30%) profiles.

## 3. Gold (Commodity Reserve)

### Purpose within Simulation
Serves as an inflation hedge and store of value during market volatility or geopolitical uncertainty.

### Simulated Characteristics
- **Expected Volatility:** Moderate (simulated annual volatility ~11.5%).
- **Historical 1-Year Simulated Return:** Non-correlated performance (simulated +9.4%).
- **Typical Role:** Diversifying commodity reserve (5% – 10% across all profiles).

## 4. International Equity (Global Developed Markets)

### Purpose within Simulation
Enhances geographical diversification and captures global growth opportunities outside domestic markets.

### Simulated Characteristics

## 5. Cash / Liquid (Short-Term Liquidity & Money Market)

### Purpose within Simulation
Ensures immediate liquidity, emergency buffer availability, and capital preservation without price volatility.

### Simulated Characteristics
- **Expected Volatility:** Very Low / Zero (simulated annual volatility ~0.5%).
- **Historical 1-Year Simulated Return:** Low risk-free yield (simulated +4.5%).
- **Typical Role:** Immediate liquidity buffer (5% standard baseline, up to 15% for High Liquidity requirement).
